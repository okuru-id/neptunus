# Neptunus v0.2.30

Music has sound again on iPhone and iPad after watching a film.

## Fixed

- 🍎 **No music sound after a film on iOS.** Playing a film while music was on took over the audio, and after closing the film the music played silently. Playing or resuming music after a film now brings the sound back.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
