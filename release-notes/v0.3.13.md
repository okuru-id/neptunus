# Neptunus v0.3.13

Streamed songs show in your library, and a short tour on first launch.

## New

- 🎵 **Streamed songs in Lagu, Album and Artis.** Songs played from the catalog and kept in the stream cache are listed with your library, marked with a small icon, and play from there. They leave the list when the cache drops them.
- 👋 **Onboarding and what's new.** The first launch shows a short introduction, and after an update the app shows once what changed.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` (mpv and ffmpeg recommended) |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
