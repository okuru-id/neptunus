# Neptunus v0.3.11

Songs from the catalog can be streamed instead of downloaded, and playlists can be made from one song.

## New

- 🎧 **Stream from the catalog.** Pick one download extension to stream with, and its quality, in Settings › Ekstensi. Tapping a song in the catalog then plays it straight away: from the library when it is already downloaded, otherwise through a temporary cache (at most 1 GB, the songs played longest ago go first) without adding it to the library. The songs after it in the list play on, the next one fetched while the current one plays.
- 🔀 **Mix.** Make a playlist of up to 30 songs from one song: its artists' songs and those of the artists they sing with. Start it from the button on a catalog song, on a song's cover on the catalog home, or with **Buat mix** in Playlist. Progress shows in the bell.
- ➕ **Save any catalog page as a playlist.** Albums, artists, the extensions' playlists and mixes have **Simpan sebagai playlist**, artwork included.
- ▶️ **Playlists stream the songs not yet downloaded.** Tap one or press Putar: the downloaded songs play first, then the rest streams. The song playing is marked like the others.
- 🗄️ **A Cache tab in Settings** for the film cache and the streamed songs, each folded until opened, with songs to pick and delete.

## Changed

- The catalog marks downloaded songs with a small check instead of a play button, rows scroll with ‹ › buttons beside their title (swipe on phones and tablets), and an album, artist or playlist opens at its top.
- A playlist's song count includes the songs not yet downloaded.
- Settings opens on Tentang, now the first tab.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` (mpv and ffmpeg recommended) |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
