# Neptunus v0.3.0

Neptunus is now one player for your music and your films, on the desktop and on your phone. This release adds a place for what you watch: films and series you save, and the ones you were in the middle of.

## New

- 🔖 **Bookmarks.** Tap **Simpan** on a film or series page to keep it. Everything saved sits in a **Disimpan** row at the top of the video catalogs.
- ⏯️ **Continue watching.** A **Lanjutkan menonton** row shows the titles you played last, with how far you got and the episode you were on ("S1E3 · sisa 24:00"). Tapping one opens its page with that episode already picked.
- ▶️ **One-tap resume.** A title you played before gets a **Lanjutkan** button on its page that plays the same source again from where you stopped.
- 🎯 **Resume follows the film, not the link.** Where you stopped is kept per film or episode, so it still resumes there after you pick another source because the old link expired.

Both rows can be tidied with the × on each poster. They are kept on each device.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
