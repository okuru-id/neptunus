# Neptunus v0.3.7

Films play far more often: phones get a real video player, and the computer app repairs what its player can't read.

## New

- 📱 **A native film player on phones.** On Android films play in ExoPlayer, on iOS in VLC's engine, instead of the WebView: MKV, HEVC, AC3/DTS and more, with the extensions' subtitles and audio tracks to pick from. The place you stopped is kept, like before.
- ▶️ **Pemutar lain on phones.** Films can open in VLC, MX Player or Just Player on Android, and VLC or Infuse on iOS, with the stream's headers and the extensions' subtitles sent along.
- 🛠️ **Films the app player refuses are repaired.** When a film has no picture or won't load within 5 seconds, ffmpeg repackages it on the fly, and HEVC 10-bit is converted so it plays in the app.

## Changed

- 💬 **Subtitles by language name.** Outside players (mpv, VLC, MX…) now list subtitles as "Inggris 2 - …" like the app's menu, not file ids, and all of them are sent, not only 30. The one you picked last shows from the start.
- ⚠️ **Clear error messages.** When a film can't be fetched, the app says what the source answered (not found, refused, server busy…) instead of a vague format error.
- 🪟🍎 **ffmpeg on Windows and macOS.** ffmpeg installed with winget, Scoop, Chocolatey, Homebrew or MacPorts is found even when the app is opened from the Start menu or Finder, runs without a console window, and the app says how to install it when it's missing.

## Fixed

- The subtitle shown as on but not appearing until picked again.
- The selected episode's border.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` (mpv and ffmpeg recommended) |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
