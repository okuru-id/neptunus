# Neptunus v0.3.14

A second look: the Nothing style, black and white with dot-matrix type, music bars of dots and an astronaut drifting behind.

## New

- ⚫ **Nothing style.** Pick it in Settings › Tampilan › Gaya: pure black and monochrome with the album art as the only colour, dot-matrix page titles and icons, mono type for durations and counts, and a red play button. Album and artist cards get a menu to play, shuffle or open them, and there is a button to shuffle everything. Material stays as it was.
- 📊 **Dot music bars for the progress.** In the Nothing style the played part of the song is columns of dots that bounce while it plays and settle on pause, with the playhead in red.
- 🧑‍🚀 **A pixel astronaut.** The Nothing style's background has a faint dot-matrix astronaut drifting between two orbits.

## Changed

- The sidebar's collapse button lines up with the menu icons, and the collapsed sidebar no longer shows a sideways scrollbar.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` (mpv and ffmpeg recommended) |
| Windows | `-setup.exe` or `.msi` |
| macOS | `_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel) |
| Android 8.0+ | `_arm64.apk` |
| iOS | `.ipa` (OTA when available) or `_unsigned.ipa`, sign it with SideStore, iloader or LiveContainer |

Update with **Unduh & pasang** in the update banner or Settings › Tentang.
